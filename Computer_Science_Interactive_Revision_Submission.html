2. OLED displays

1(a). Many mobile phone and tablet manufacturers are moving to OLED screen technology. Give three reasons why this is happening.

1(b). A television manufacturer claims: “Our OLED screens allow the user to enjoy over one million vivid colours in true-to-life vision.” Comment on the validity of this claim.

3. Games console: ROM, EPROM, RAM and USB

A company is developing a new games console. The game will be stored on a ROM chip once the program has been fully tested and developed.

2(a)(i). Give two advantages of putting the game’s program on a ROM chip.

2(a)(ii). Explain why the manufacturers would use an EPROM chip during development.

2(a)(iii). Explain why the manufacturers are also using RAM chips on the internal circuit board.

2(a)(iv). Apart from connecting games controllers, give reasons why four USB ports are incorporated.

2(b). During development, plastic parts are made using a 3D printer. Give two reasons why the manufacturer would use 3D printers.
